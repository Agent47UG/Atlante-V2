 ATLANTE — IDEAS & ADD-ONS
 A running list of ~40 changes, features, and polish items grounded in the
 current codebase (React + Three.js + Vite, data in src/data/new-data/).
 Rough effort tags:  [S] small  [M] medium  [L] large
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 A. SHARING & STATE / URL
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1.  [S] Deep-link URLs — sync App.tsx state to query params (?civ=, ?year=,
        ?story=, ?node=) so any view can be shared and restored on reload.
2.  [S] "Copy link" button on the story player and civilization view.
3.  [M] Persist user-built dynamic stories to localStorage (they currently live
        only in React state and vanish on refresh).
4.  [S] Remember last-visited year / civilization between sessions.
5.  [S] Browser back/forward support (history.pushState) for civ + story steps.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 B. KNOWLEDGE GRAPH & DATA
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6.  [L] Neo4j / Wikidata phase — the graph.ts header already flags this as the
        roadmap; pull live data or expand well beyond the current ~250 nodes.
7.  [M] Deepen thin regions — Americas (Maya/Aztec/Inca), Africa (Mali, Aksum,
        Great Zimbabwe), SE Asia (Khmer, Srivijaya) are under-represented.
8.  [S] Surface NodeDetails everywhere — glyph, era, born/died, contributions,
        relatedPeople are defined in types.ts but barely shown in the UI.
9.  [M] Edge legend / styling — color or dash edges by verb (invented, inspired,
        influenced) so the constellation reads at a glance.
10. [S] Validate data at build time — a script asserting every story nodeId and
        every edge source/target exists in NODES (catch typos early).
11. [M] Node "importance" weighting — size/brightness by degree so hubs stand
        out (you already compute neighborsOf in graphView).
12. [M] Bidirectional cross-civ highlighting — hovering a bridge node pulses the
        civilizations it connects on the globe.
13. [S] Add source/citation field to KnowledgeNode for scholarly credibility.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 C. STORIES
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
14. [M] Story autoplay — play/pause that auto-advances steps on a timer, turning
        a story into a guided "documentary" mode.
15. [S] Progress indicator — step dots / "3 of 7" in the story player.
16. [S] Prev/Next also on-screen (not just arrow keys) for touch users.
17. [M] Global node search — reuse matchNodes() from storyBuilder to power a
        jump-to-node search box, independent of story building.
18. [M] Smarter story builder — rank multiple candidate paths and pick the most
        "narrative" one; today it takes the first shortest path.
19. [S] Save / favorite built stories into the suggested list.
20. [S] Randomize / "Surprise me" story button.
21. [M] Shareable story export (image card or text summary of the path).

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 D. GLOBE & TIMELINE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
22. [M] Timeline filtering — dim/disable civilizations whose periods don't cover
        the current year (period data already exists in civilizations.ts).
23. [S] Timeline era labels / ticks that name the age as you scrub.
24. [M] Animated "play through time" — auto-scrub the timeline and watch civs
        rise and fall on the globe.
25. [S] Show a civilization's lifespan bar when it's selected.
26. [M] Trade-route / connection arcs drawn on the globe between linked civs.
27. [S] Day/night terminator or subtle atmosphere shader for depth.
28. [S] Click-empty-space to deselect / fly home (discoverability).
